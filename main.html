Sudhanshu Agrawal

ML Research Engineer at Qualcomm AI Research

Hi everyone! My name is Sudhanshu and I'm an ML Research Engineer at Qualcomm AI. I work on LLM efficiency research supervised by Mingu Lee. Our focus is inventing new speculative decoding algorithms for edge applications - making LLMs fast enough to run locally on your phone or laptop! I graduated from UCLA with a double major in Computer Science and Mathematics. I was fortunate to do research with Professor Aditya Grover on generative modeling and with Professor Levon Nurbekyan on mean-field games. I'm always interested in talking about LLM efficiency, generative modeling, and foundation models. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to chat!
